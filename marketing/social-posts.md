## C. Explainer posts (one fact each, any order)

### Post C1: The deadlines

> Three receipt apps, three deadlines. Shping takes a receipt for 7 days. ShopBack takes it for 28. ReceiptJar's rules are in its app. ReceiptRelay shows the days left for each one on every receipt you scan.

### Post C2: The caps

> Shping allows 2 receipts a day from one store, 5 from one store in 7 days and 100 a month. ReceiptRelay counts them for you and says which rule a receipt fails before you upload it.

### Post C3: One receipt, one claim per app

> ShopBack accepts one receipt per purchase, however you send it. ReceiptRelay gives each photo a fingerprint and stops the same receipt going to the same app twice.

### Post C4: Where the receipts live

> ReceiptRelay stores your receipts on your phone, encrypted. It never asks for your Shping, ShopBack or ReceiptJar password; you submit in each app yourself.

### Post C5: How the app is paid for

> ReceiptRelay is free. It shows one ad after you submit a receipt and an optional ad you can watch to support it. Ad code never runs on a receipt screen.

### Post C6: What it is not

> ReceiptRelay doesn't read receipts with AI and doesn't upload for you. It checks fixed rules written from each app's published terms, and every no comes with its reason, such as "over 7 days for Shping".
