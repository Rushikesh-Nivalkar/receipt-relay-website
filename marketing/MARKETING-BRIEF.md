# ReceiptRelay marketing brief

Status: prepared 2026-11-23 by marketing-agent (idea-workflow step 13). Nothing is published. The website stays local, there is no domain, and the app is not built yet.

## The core message

Receipt rewards app for Australian shoppers. Scan a shopping receipt once. ReceiptRelay checks it against Shping, ShopBack and ReceiptJar rules and guides each claim. Free on Android.
