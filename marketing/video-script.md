# Promo video script

Status: script only. Film it once the app is built: a screen recording of the real app plus one hand-held phone shot. No AI-generated video, voice or images. Structure from video-use: audio first, cuts on word boundaries, each on-screen card held at least 3 seconds, the last frame held 1 second or more. Subtitles in sentence case.

Format: 30 seconds, 9:16 for Reels and Shorts, with a 16:9 crop for the website and Play listing. Voice-over by the developer, or on-screen text only.

## 30-second version

| Time | Picture | Voice-over | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:04 | Hand holds a supermarket receipt over a kitchen bench; the phone camera opens in ReceiptRelay | "One shopping receipt. Three receipt apps." | Receipt rewards app for Australia |
| 0:04 to 0:09 | Screen recording: the photo is taken; store, date and total typed in | "Scan it once in ReceiptRelay. Pick the store, type the date and total." | Scan a receipt once |
| 0:09 to 0:15 | The rule check screen: Shping, ShopBack and ReceiptJar rows, days left in the system monospace; one row shows a reason | "It checks the receipt against each app's published rules. Shping allows 7 days. ShopBack allows 28. Every no comes with its reason." | Shping 7 days · ShopBack 28 days |
| 0:15 to 0:21 | ShopBack opens from the share screen; then the Shping steps card | "Then it hands the photo to each app, and you submit it there. It never asks for your passwords." | You submit in each app |
| 0:21 to 0:26 | Claim list: submitted, pending, paid; a reminder notification slides in | "Each claim stays in one list, with a reminder before the deadline." | Days left for every app |
| 0:26 to 0:30 | Glass app icon on the slate ground, then the knockout mark on olive with the button text | "ReceiptRelay. Free on Android." | Download for Android. Not affiliated with Shping, ShopBack or ReceiptJar. |

Word count of the voice-over: 76 words, about 28 seconds at a normal speaking pace.

## Shot notes

- Use a receipt from the developer's own shopping, with no card number, loyalty number or name readable. Blur the barcode.
- Provider apps appear only as Android's share screen and the step text; no screens from inside Shping, ShopBack or ReceiptJar, no provider logos in close-up.
- Mock data in the app must not show earnings totals.
- Colours and type in cards: `brand/tokens.css` (slate `#A6B3BC`, ink `#1C1C1C`, olive `#2C3620`, text on olive `#E3E2DA`), the system font for words (Roboto on Android) and the system monospace for numbers.
- The last card shows the "not affiliated" line for at least 1 second.
- Music: a track with a licence for commercial use, credited in the description.
