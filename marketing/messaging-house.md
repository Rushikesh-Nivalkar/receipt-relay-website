# ReceiptRelay messaging house

Status: prepared; unpublished. Built on `positioning.md`. Every line below uses only the fact sheet in `docs/WEBSITE-PLAN.md` section 5. Use these lines word for word across the site, the store listing, posts and the video.

## Roof: the core message

**Receipt rewards app for Australian shoppers.**

Scan a shopping receipt once. ReceiptRelay checks it against Shping, ShopBack and ReceiptJar rules and guides each claim. Free on Android.

Brand line (one per piece at most, never as a title): Scan once, claim with each app.

## Pillars

### 1. Rule check per app

- **Message:** ReceiptRelay checks each receipt against each app's published rules before you upload.
- **Proof:** Shping's 7-day limit, 2 receipts a day per store, 5 per store in 7 days and 100 a month. ShopBack's 28-day limit and one receipt per purchase. ReceiptJar's rules are shown in its app.
- **Short form:** Every no comes with its reason.
- **Example line:** "Not eligible: over 7 days for Shping."

### 2. Guided upload into each provider's own app

- **Message:** ReceiptRelay hands the original photo to each app, and you submit it there.
- **Proof:** ShopBack opens through Android's share screen; Shping and ReceiptJar get the photo saved to your gallery and the steps on screen. Then you mark it sent.
- **Short form:** You submit in each app; ReceiptRelay never logs in for you.

### 3. Claim tracking and reminders

- **Message:** Each claim stays in one list until it is paid, rejected or expired.
- **Proof:** statuses run from submitted to pending to paid; a reminder goes out before a receipt gets too old for an app; the same receipt is caught before it goes to the same app twice; each app's paid total adds up month by month.
- **Short form:** Days left for every app, in one list.

### 4. Receipts stay on your phone

- **Message:** Receipts are stored on your phone, encrypted, and never sent to ad networks.
- **Proof:** encrypted storage on the device; no provider passwords asked for or stored; ad code never runs on a receipt screen.
- **Short form:** Your receipts stay on your phone.

## Foundation: facts every piece may lean on

- Free. One ad after you submit a receipt, plus an optional ad you can watch to support the app. No banners, no pop-ups.
- Fixed rules written as code. No AI.
- Android only, Australia only. iPhone not yet.
- Not affiliated with Shping, ShopBack or ReceiptJar. Product names are trademarks of their owners.

## Message by audience stage

| Reader | Lead with | Then |
|---|---|---|
| Uses one receipt app | Pillar 1: the other apps' rules side by side | Free, Android |
| Uses two or three apps | Pillar 3: days left per app in one list | Pillar 1 |
| Got a rejection over a cap | Pillar 1: the reason before upload | Pillar 3 |
| Worried about privacy | Pillar 4 | The ads line from the foundation |
| Asks "what's the catch" | The ads line, word for word | Pillar 4 |

## Lines that must never appear

- "Certified", "ISO 27001 certified", "SOC 2 certified", "bank-grade".
- "Partner", "official", "approved by" next to any provider name.
- "Guaranteed", "never get banned", "earn more", any dollar figure for earnings.
- User counts, ratings, reviews, testimonials, "join thousands".
- "Automatic upload", "logs in for you", "AI", "reads your receipt".
- A number for ReceiptJar's limits until its in-app rules are confirmed.
