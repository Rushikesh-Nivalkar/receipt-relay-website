How it works

Take one photo of a receipt. ReceiptRelay checks it against each app's published rules, then hands the original photo to that app, so you submit it yourself, once per app, before its deadline.

Checks each app's rules before you upload

The check is fixed rules, not guesswork: receipt age, daily and weekly caps, and one claim per purchase. A no always comes with its reason.

Questions

What people ask before they install.

Email us a question

How does ReceiptRelay make money?

From ads: one after you submit a receipt and an optional ad you can watch to support the app. No banners, no pop-ups.

Do ad networks see my receipts?

No. Receipts stay on your phone, encrypted, and the ad code never runs on a receipt screen.

Does it log in to Shping or ShopBack for me?

No. It never asks for those passwords. It hands the photo to each app and you submit it there.

Can I send one receipt to all three apps?

Yes, once to each app, if your receipt meets each app's own rules. The rules are each app's, and they can change.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. ReceiptRelay is on Android first, in Australia only.
