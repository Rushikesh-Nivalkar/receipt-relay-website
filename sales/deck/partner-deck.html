04 · What ReceiptRelay does

Captures once, checks the rules, tracks the claim

01

Captures the receipt

A photo, PDF or shared image, kept as the original file. The shopper picks the store and types the date and total.

02

Checks published rules

Eligible, Not eligible with its reason, or Check manually when a rule is unknown. Fixed rules, no AI.

03

Hands off and tracks

The shopper submits in your app. Reminders before deadlines; each claim tracked to Paid, Rejected or Expired.

06 · Designed for your programme

Your rules checked before the upload

  • Checks your published age limit and caps before the shopper uploads.
  • Sends each receipt to each provider once: a SHA-256 fingerprint blocks the same file going to the same provider twice.
  • Passes on the original file, untouched.
  • Sends the shopper to your app, where your review and your rewards apply.
Design facts. Results come after the beta.06

07 · Data and privacy

Receipts stay on the shopper's phone

  • Encrypted at rest: SQLCipher database, AES-256-GCM files, keys in Android Keystore.
  • Receipts are never uploaded to our servers. The ad code never runs on a receipt screen.
  • TLS 1.3 with certificate pinning, and no plaintext logs.
  • Built towards ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and SOC 2 controls. Not certified: no audit has taken place.

08 · How it is funded

Free for shoppers, funded by one ad

  • One native ad on the "Submitted" screen, plus an optional rewarded "support us" ad. No banners or interstitials.
  • Google UMP consent with a non-personalised option, and a "How we make money" screen in onboarding and settings.
Planning assumption

About 24 native impressions per user a month at about US$3 eCPM (a global vendor figure; Australian rates unknown) comes to about US$0.07 per user a month.

10 · What we are asking for

Five questions for your team

  1. Written confirmation that a genuine receipt may go once to your programme, also when the shopper submits it to other programmes.
